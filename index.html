Flybridge Investment Theses

How AI learns, and where it keeps what it learns

Two theses on the infrastructure underneath every agent. One asks how a model keeps learning after it ships. The other asks where an agent keeps its work while it does the job.

Jason Rubenstein Flybridge
Scroll

Frontier labs are racing toward systems that can build their own successors. Two bottlenecks stand in the way. Models forget nearly as fast as they learn, so every deployed system runs on knowledge frozen at ship date. And agents have no durable place to keep context, show their work, or undo a mistake, so every long task starts to strain against the tools built for humans. These theses look at the startups forming around each problem, and where the durable value is likely to land.

01 · Model Learning

Continual Learning

Billions of weights freeze the moment training stops. Continual learning keeps turning those dials after ship day. It ranks among the hardest unsolved problems in AI, and it gates the path to recursive self improvement.
